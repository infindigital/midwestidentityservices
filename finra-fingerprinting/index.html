About this service

FINRA fingerprinting for securities professionals

FINRA requires fingerprinting as part of the background check process for broker-dealers, registered representatives and other securities industry professionals.

Midwest Identity Services provides professional FD-258 ink fingerprinting completed to the standards required for FINRA submissions, including proper rolling technique, required field completion and quality review before every card leaves our office.

If your sponsoring firm or compliance department has instructed you to complete FINRA fingerprinting as part of a Form U4 or Form U5 filing, this service is designed to meet those requirements. Appointments are available for individual applicants and can be coordinated for multiple registrants from the same firm.

FINRA submissions require heightened accuracy.

Poor ridge clarity, incorrect field completion or improper card handling can result in delays or rejections. We follow proper technique and review every card before you leave, call us at (816) 442-0295 and bring any instructions provided by your firm or sponsor.

Who needs this service

Financial professionals who need fingerprinting

If your sponsoring firm, employer, regulator or compliance department has directed you to complete fingerprinting, these are the most common situations we handle.

  • New registrants

    Individuals entering the securities industry for the first time and registering as a representative with a FINRA member firm. Fingerprints are required as part of the initial Form U4 filing and background check process.

  • Transferring representatives

    Registered representatives moving from one member firm to another who are required to submit updated fingerprints as part of the transfer and re-registration process with the new sponsoring firm.

  • Broker-dealer principals

    Supervisory and principal-level individuals at broker-dealer firms required to complete FINRA fingerprinting as part of their own registration or as part of the firm’s overall compliance process.

  • Firms with multiple registrants

    Financial firms that need to coordinate fingerprinting for multiple employees simultaneously, new hires, transfers or compliance renewals. We schedule group appointments to process all registrants efficiently.

  • Investment adviser representatives

    Investment adviser representatives registering at the state or federal level who are required to provide fingerprints as part of the adviser registration and background check requirements.

  • Compliance-directed submissions

    Individuals directed by their firm’s compliance department or legal counsel to complete fingerprinting in connection with any FINRA regulatory requirement, disciplinary matter or background review.

  • Banks & credit unions

    Bank and credit union employees asked to provide fingerprints for pre-employment screening or regulatory background checks. We complete FD-258 cards to your employer’s instructions, individually or for a whole branch team.

  • Executives & board members

    Officers, directors and senior hires whose fingerprints are needed for a license application, a change of control filing or an FBI Identity History Summary. We can come to your office so executives do not lose half a day.

  • Insurance & other financial licenses

    Insurance producers, adjusters and other licensed financial professionals. Some regulators accept FD-258 cards and others require electronic submission through a specific vendor, so send us your instructions and we will confirm the right method first.

What to expect

The FINRA fingerprinting process

Most appointments take 15–20 minutes. Accuracy and quality are prioritized at every step to minimize the chance of rejection.

  1. Book your appointment & confirm requirements

    Book online or call (816) 442-0295. If your firm has provided specific instructions, a CRD number or a reference form, have those ready. Calling ahead lets us confirm any firm-specific requirements before you arrive.

  2. Identity verification & intake

    We verify your government-issued photo ID and collect all required applicant information, full legal name, date of birth, Social Security number and any firm-provided reference numbers. We review your firm’s instructions at this stage so all fields are completed correctly from the start.

  3. Ink fingerprint capture on FD-258 cards

    Using professional fingerprinting ink and technique, we roll all ten fingerprints onto the FD-258 card. FINRA submissions require heightened attention to rolling technique, ink coverage and ridge legibility.

  4. Quality review before you leave

    Every completed card is reviewed under proper lighting for ridge clarity, complete impressions and correct field information. If any print or field does not meet quality standards, we redo it on the spot, no extra charge.

  5. Cards in hand, submit per your firm’s instructions

    You leave with completed, quality-reviewed FD-258 cards ready to submit. Whether you deliver them to your sponsoring firm or submit them directly as instructed by your compliance department, the cards are ready to go. We do not submit to FINRA on your behalf.

FINRA fingerprinting FAQ

Frequently asked questions

More questions? Call (816) 442-0295 and we will answer in two minutes.

Why does FINRA require fingerprinting?

FINRA requires fingerprinting as part of its background check process for securities industry professionals. Under FINRA Rule 3110(e), member firms must investigate the background of associated persons, and fingerprinting is required by the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 for certain categories of personnel. The fingerprints are used to check criminal history records through the FBI.

Do you fingerprint bank employees, executives and insurance professionals too?

Yes. Beyond FINRA registrants, we fingerprint bank and credit union staff, executives and board members, insurance producers and other financial professionals. Requirements differ by employer and regulator: some accept FD-258 cards, others require electronic submission through a specific vendor. Send us your instructions and we will confirm the correct method before your appointment.

Do you submit fingerprints to FINRA on my behalf?

No. We provide the completed FD-258 fingerprint cards only. Submission to FINRA is handled by you, your sponsoring firm or your compliance department, following the instructions they have provided. We do not submit on your behalf, control processing timelines or have access to FINRA’s systems.

How many FD-258 cards do I need for FINRA?

Most FINRA submissions require two FD-258 fingerprint cards per individual. However, some firms or situations may require additional copies. Check with your sponsoring firm or compliance department before your appointment and let us know when you book so we prepare accordingly.

What does FINRA fingerprinting cost?

FINRA fingerprinting uses FD-258 cards: $45, plus $20 for each additional card. Cards and a mailing envelope are supplied. Mobile appointments add a travel fee quoted before booking, and we confirm all costs before your appointment begins.

What information do I need to have ready for the appointment?

You will need your full legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, and any firm-provided reference numbers or CRD numbers. Also bring your valid government-issued photo ID and any instructions or forms provided by your sponsoring firm or compliance department.

Can my firm schedule fingerprinting for several registrants at once?

Yes. We accommodate coordinated appointments for multiple registrants from the same firm, either at our office or via mobile service at your firm’s location. Each individual is processed separately with their own completed, reviewed FD-258 cards. Firms with ongoing hiring can also set up a corporate fingerprinting program.

Do you offer mobile fingerprinting at our office or for our executives?

Yes. We offer mobile fingerprinting at firm offices across the Kansas City metro, and elsewhere in Missouri and Kansas by arrangement. It is particularly useful for executives and for firms coordinating several registrants. Mobile pricing is the per-person price plus a travel fee quoted before booking. Read about mobile fingerprinting for executives.

I am registering in more than one state. Do I need to be fingerprinted for each one?

Not always. Our digital print-to-card system can capture your fingerprints once and produce multiple FD-258 or agency-specific cards when permitted by the receiving agency. Multi-state print-to-card is $60, plus $25 for each additional printed card. See multi-state fingerprinting.

My previous FINRA submission was rejected, can you help?

Yes. Bring the rejection notice when you come in, it will cite the specific reason the submission was rejected. We will prepare new FD-258 cards with direct attention to the cited issue, whether that is poor ridge clarity, incorrect field completion or card handling problems. We review every card before you leave.
