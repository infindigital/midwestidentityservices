About the Requirements Center

Requirements FAQ

Can’t find your situation? Call (816) 442-0295.

What should I send for a requirement review?

Whatever the requesting agency, board or employer gave you: the instruction sheet or letter, any ORI number or reason code, application requirements and any cards they supplied. A photo or screenshot is fine.

Can you guarantee my agency will accept my fingerprints?

No one can promise that on an agency’s behalf, because the receiving agency controls final acceptance. What we can do is check your instructions before your appointment, capture high-quality prints and prepare them in the format the agency asks for.

Do all states accept the same fingerprint card or submission method?

No. Some accept FD-258 cards, some require their own card, and some require Live Scan or a state-specific electronic submission. That is why we review each state’s instructions before multi-state appointments.

How current is this information?

Each guide shows the date it was last reviewed. Agencies change their rules, so if your instructions differ from what you read here, follow your instructions and let us know.
