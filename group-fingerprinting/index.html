About this service

On-site fingerprinting for teams & organizations

Designed for situations where fingerprinting is required by a regulatory, access, licensing or compliance requirement — and processing staff individually off-site would be impractical.

Midwest Identity Services provides on-site group fingerprinting for organizations that need multiple employees fingerprinted efficiently, accurately and with minimal disruption to daily operations.

We arrive at your location with all necessary equipment and materials, and process your entire team during a single scheduled visit. Every individual receives properly completed FD-258 fingerprint cards, reviewed for quality before we leave. For one person, see our individual services page.

Recently mandated by an external agency or authority?

If a new contract, licensing requirement, federal access clearance or facility regulation has introduced fingerprinting requirements for your staff, this service is built for exactly that. Call (816) 442-0295 and we will walk you through the process and scheduling in under two minutes.

How it works

Group fingerprinting — step by step

Group appointments are typically completed significantly faster than sending employees individually to a fingerprinting office.

  1. Call or book your group appointment

    Contact us at (816) 442-0295 or book online. Let us know how many staff members need fingerprinting, the type of submission required (FD-258 for a specific agency or board), and your location. We will confirm availability and scheduling logistics upfront.

  2. Confirm location details & staff list

    We confirm your address, the total number of participants, the designated processing area and any agency-specific requirements. For HR-managed appointments, we coordinate directly with your team to ensure participants arrive in sequence and are prepared.

  3. We arrive fully equipped

    Our certified technician arrives with all fingerprinting equipment and current FD-258 card revisions. All you need to provide is a flat surface, two chairs and reasonable lighting. We set up and break down completely.

  4. Each staff member is processed individually

    Each person presents their valid government-issued photo ID, provides their information for the FD-258 form and has their fingerprints taken. We work through the group consecutively at a steady pace — approximately 10–15 minutes per person including ID verification and completion of the form.

  5. Quality reviewed & cards delivered on-site

    Every completed FD-258 card is reviewed for ridge clarity and correct identifier information before we move to the next person. Completed cards are delivered to each individual, or to your HR coordinator, before we leave your site.

Group fingerprinting FAQ

Frequently asked questions

More questions? Call (816) 442-0295 and we will answer in two minutes.

What is the minimum group size for this service?

There is no strict minimum — we accommodate groups of any size, from 2 staff members up to 30 or more. However, on-site group service is most cost-effective for groups of 5 or more. For smaller groups or individuals, visiting our office at 8101 E. Bannister Rd is usually the faster and more affordable option. Call us and we will advise on the best approach.

How long does a group fingerprinting appointment take?

Groups of 5–10 staff members are often completed in under one hour. Allow approximately 10–15 minutes per person including ID verification, intake and fingerprinting. We will give you a time estimate based on your group size when you schedule. Larger groups of 15–30+ staff take proportionally longer.

Do you come to our location or do staff visit your office?

For group fingerprinting, we come to your location. We travel to your office, facility, warehouse, healthcare building, school or job site anywhere across the Kansas City metro area. Your staff are processed on-site — no employee travel required. A travel fee applies and is confirmed at booking.

What does each staff member need to bring?

Every participant must have a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID — driver’s licence, state ID or U.S. passport. They should also know the name of the agency or licensing board they are submitting to, and bring any forms or ORI numbers provided by that agency. We provide all fingerprinting materials.

What space do you need at our location?

A table, two chairs and good lighting — that is all. Any quiet indoor space works: a conference room, break room, reception area or lobby. We set up and break down our own equipment completely. We have also worked in covered outdoor spaces when indoor access is not available.

Do different staff members need fingerprints for different agencies?

Yes — we handle mixed submissions in the same appointment. Each person’s FD-258 card is completed to the specifications of their specific requesting agency, including the correct ORI number and reason fingerprinted. Let us know about mixed submissions when you schedule so we can prepare accordingly.

What does group fingerprinting cost?

Cost is $60–$99 based on services needed, per staff member. A travel fee also applies based on your distance from our office — this is confirmed upfront at booking, no surprises. Call (816) 442-0295 with your location and group size and we will provide a full quote immediately.

Our organization was recently required to submit fingerprints — how do we start?

Call us at (816) 442-0295 with the name of the agency that is requiring fingerprints and the number of staff members involved. We will confirm exactly what is needed — form type, number of fingerprint card sets per person and any specific requirements from that agency — and get you scheduled quickly. This is a very common situation and we handle it regularly.
