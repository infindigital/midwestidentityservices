Good to know

ATF eFile fingerprinting FAQ

More questions? Call (816) 442-0295 and we will answer in two minutes.

Do you do fingerprints for ATF Form 1 and Form 4?

Yes. For NFA applications filed through ATF eForms, we fingerprint individuals and gun-trust responsible persons for Form 1 (making an SBR or other NFA item) and Form 4 (transferring a suppressor or other NFA item). See also ATF & FFL fingerprinting.

What is the difference between electronic ATF prints and ink cards?

Electronic ATF prints are captured digitally on certified equipment and delivered in ATF-ready electronic format, with far fewer rejections for print quality, and no ink, mailing or lost cards. Ink FD-258 cards have to be mailed in and you wait, with a real risk of rejection for smudging or overfilled prints. A rejected ink card can add months to your NFA timeline, since each responsible person’s card has to be handled and potentially redone separately.

Can you fingerprint everyone on a gun trust?

Yes. Every responsible person listed on a gun trust needs their own fingerprints submitted as part of the eForm 1 or eForm 4 application. We can fingerprint the whole group in one clean visit, each responsible person receives their own electronic, ATF-ready prints, so nothing gets handled separately or delayed.

What do I need to bring to my appointment?

Bring a valid government-issued photo ID, and let us know your eForm type (Form 1 or Form 4) when you book. If you are filing for a gun trust, bring the same ID for each responsible person who needs to be fingerprinted. Most appointments take about fifteen minutes per person.

Do you work with FFL/SOT dealers and their customers?

Yes. FFL and Class 3 SOT dealers regularly send customers to us for the fingerprints required on their NFA paperwork, both for individual customers and responsible persons on gun trusts. If you are a dealer with regular fingerprinting needs for your customers, call us to discuss setting up a streamlined process for your business.
