How an appointment actually runs

Most people are in and out in 15 to 30 minutes. Here is what happens either side of that.

  1. Tell us what the agency asked for

    Book online or call. Tell us who is requesting the prints — a licensing board, the ATF, FINRA, an employer or an out-of-state agency — and we confirm whether you need Live Scan or an FD-258 ink card.

  2. Bring photo ID and any agency forms

    A driver’s licence, passport or state ID, plus any forms, ORI number or instruction sheet the agency sent you. If you are unsure what you have, bring it all and we will sort it out at the counter.

  3. Prints are captured and checked

    Live Scan takes a few minutes per person. Ink cards are rolled by hand and reviewed for legibility before you leave, which is where most rejections are caught.

  4. Submission or hand-off

    Electronic submissions go out through our authorized channeling partner, with FBI Identity History Summary results typically back in 24–72 hours. Ink cards are handed to you ready to mail, or sent on where the agency allows it.

What should I bring for my fingerprinting appointment?

Please bring a valid government-issued photo ID such as a driver’s license, passport, or state ID. If your agency provided fingerprinting forms or instructions, bring those as well.

Do I need an appointment for fingerprinting services?

Walk-ins are welcome for most services during business hours. However, appointments are recommended for mobile fingerprinting, large groups, and ATF-related services.

How long does the fingerprinting process take?

Most fingerprinting appointments take between 15–30 minutes depending on the service type and documentation requirements.

Do you provide FBI background checks?

Yes. We offer FBI Identity History Summary checks through authorized electronic submission partners for faster processing and secure results. Read more about FBI background checks and apostille services.

Do you offer mobile fingerprinting services?

Yes. We provide on-site mobile fingerprinting services for businesses, healthcare facilities, staffing agencies, schools, and large organizations across the Kansas City metro area.

What types of fingerprinting services do you provide?

We provide Live Scan fingerprinting, FD-258 ink card fingerprinting, ATF & FFL fingerprinting, FBI background checks, employment screening, and bulk fingerprinting services. Compare the methods if you are not sure which one applies.
