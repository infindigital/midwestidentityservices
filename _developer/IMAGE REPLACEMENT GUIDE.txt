MIDWEST IDENTITY SERVICES WEBSITE
Image replacement guide
============================================================

Every photo on the website is a temporary stock image, chosen to show the
right subject at the right shape. Replace them with your own photos whenever
you are ready: no page needs to be edited.

HOW TO REPLACE A PHOTO
----------------------

  1. Pick the photo below that you want to change.
  2. Save your new picture using EXACTLY the file name shown as
     "Replace the file". Keep the same file extension (.jpg or .png).
  3. Put it into the folder:  _developer/image-sources/
     (overwrite the file that is already there)
  4. Ask your developer to run:  npm run images

  That one command re-cuts your picture into every size the website needs
  and updates all the pages that use it.

A few tips
----------

- Send the largest version you have. Big pictures can be made smaller
  without losing quality; small ones cannot be made larger.
- Match the shape shown for each photo. A portrait photo put in a landscape
  slot will be cropped at the top and bottom.
- Keep the important part of the picture near the middle, so it survives
  cropping on phones.
- Do not use pictures of an FBI seal, badge or government logo. They suggest
  an official affiliation the business does not have.
- Make sure you have permission to use every photo of a real person or a
  real workplace.

SOCIAL SHARING PICTURE
============================================================

assets/images/social/og-image.jpg is the picture that appears when someone
shares a link to the site on Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp or in a text
message. It is generated from the "digital fingerprint" photo above, so
replacing that photo replaces this one too.
