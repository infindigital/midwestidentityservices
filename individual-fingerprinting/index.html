ATF & FFL fingerprinting

NFA applications, gun trusts & FFL fingerprinting

The ATF requires fingerprints for a range of firearms-related applications. We specialize in ATF fingerprinting and understand exactly what each form requires, from NFA Form 1 SBR builds to FFL Responsible Person submissions.

  • NFA Form 1

    Application to make and register: SBRs, suppressors and other NFA items.

  • NFA Form 4

    Tax-paid transfer, suppressors, SBRs and other NFA items.

  • NFA Form 5

    Tax-exempt transfer applications.

  • FFL Licence (Form 23)

    Every Responsible Person named on the application.

  • Gun trust members

    All Responsible Persons on the trust, scheduled together.

  • EFT electronic file

    ATF eForms uploads: see ATF eFile fingerprinting.

Multi-trustee gun trusts welcome.

All Responsible Persons can be scheduled in a single appointment. ATF / EFT fingerprinting is $75 per transaction. Call ahead to confirm which ATF form you are filing so we can prepare accordingly.

Foreign embassy & visa fingerprinting

Foreign embassies, consulates and immigration authorities often require U.S.-issued fingerprints or FBI Identity History Summaries as part of visa, residency, work permit and apostille applications. We provide both Live Scan electronic submission and FD-258 ink fingerprints for international use.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Call (816) 442-0295, we will sort it out in under two minutes.

Do I need an appointment or can I come in?

Appointments are available for most services Monday through Friday 9 AM–6 PM and Saturday 10 AM–3 PM. For ATF fingerprinting or multi-person gun trust appointments, calling ahead is recommended so we can confirm which form you are filing and prepare accordingly.

What is the difference between Live Scan and FD-258 ink fingerprints?

Live Scan captures digital fingerprints and submits them electronically, faster, no ink. FD-258 forms are traditional physical ink fingerprints on paper. Many state licensing boards, the ATF and some out-of-state agencies still require the physical fingerprints. If you are not sure which one your agency needs, compare the methods or call us and we will confirm.

How fast do FBI results come back?

FBI Identity History Summary results are often returned within 24–72 hours of electronic submission. Timing is set by the FBI and can be longer.

Do you fingerprint for ATF gun trust applications?

Yes. We routinely fingerprint multi-trustee gun trusts. All Responsible Persons can be scheduled in a single appointment. See our dedicated ATF / FFL fingerprinting page for details on Form 1, Form 4, Form 5 and FFL Form 23.

What ID do I need to bring?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID, driver’s licence, state ID or U.S. passport.

What does fingerprinting cost?

Pricing varies by service. FD-258 fingerprint cards are $45 for the first card and $20 for each additional card. Multi-State Print-to-Card is $60 for the first card and $25 for each additional printed card. FBI Identity History Summary Electronic Submission is $90. ATF / EFT fingerprinting is $75 per transaction. Call (816) 442-0295 or check our booking page for current rates on other services.

I am not sure which service I need, can you help?

Absolutely. Call us at (816) 442-0295 before you come in. Tell us what agency is asking for fingerprints and we will confirm exactly what you need to bring, usually takes less than two minutes.
