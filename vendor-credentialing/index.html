About this service

Credentialing & compliance documentation support

Credentialing requirements typically include background checks, fingerprinting, drug testing, identity verification and documentation review before access or work can begin.

Vendor credentialing is a standard process used to confirm qualification and compliance so individuals and businesses can meet onboarding requirements set by employers, healthcare systems, staffing agencies and regulated organizations.

Midwest Identity Services provides vendor credentialing support focused on documentation readiness and background check process completion, helping reduce delays caused by missing, incomplete or improperly executed materials during onboarding.

Important: we support, we do not decide.

We do not approve, deny or adjudicate credentialing decisions. Our role is to help ensure required materials are properly completed, executed and ready for review by the requesting organization. All final determinations are made by the employer, facility or governing authority.

Who this service is for

If you have been asked to complete credentialing requirements before starting work, accessing a facility or fulfilling a contract, this service helps you get it done correctly the first time.

  • Healthcare professionals

  • Travel & contract nurses

  • Independent contractors

  • Employers onboarding regulated workers

Why delays happen

Common reasons credentialing gets delayed

Most credentialing delays are caused by documentation errors, not the person’s qualifications. Completing required steps correctly the first time helps reduce re-submissions.

  • Missing fingerprints

    Fingerprints not submitted, submitted on the wrong form version, or rejected for poor print quality, requiring a complete restart of that step.

  • Missing drug test results

    Drug testing not completed before the deadline, results sent to the wrong recipient, or results lacking proper chain-of-custody documentation.

  • Incomplete onboarding packets

    Required forms returned with missing fields, unsigned pages, incorrect dates or insufficient identification documentation, causing the entire packet to be returned for correction.

  • Incorrect routing

    Required materials sent to the wrong office, department or contact, or missing the ORI number or agency-specific routing information that directs the submission correctly.

  • Licence documentation gaps

    Professional licence information missing, expired or not verified in the format required by the credentialing organization, stalling the entire approval process.

  • Missing signatures or notarizations

    Authorization forms unsigned, dates missing or notarization requirements overlooked, requiring the applicant to restart the submission process from scratch.

How it works

The vendor credentialing support process

Bring your instructions and credentialing packet. We work through each required step with you in a single appointment.

  1. Gather your credentialing materials before you arrive

    Collect everything your employer, agency or contracting organization has provided, onboarding packets, checklists, forms and any instructions specifying which steps are required. Call (816) 442-0295 if you are unsure what to bring.

  2. Documentation review at intake

    We review your credentialing packet and instructions at the start of your appointment to identify which steps need to be completed, fingerprinting, drug testing, identity verification, form completion, or a combination.

  3. Complete required steps in sequence

    We work through each required step: fingerprinting if needed (FD-258 cards prepared to agency specifications), drug or alcohol testing collection, identity verification, and form completion or review.

  4. Form & packet completion review

    Before your appointment ends, we review completed forms and documentation for missing fields, unsigned pages, incorrect dates or routing information gaps. This clerical review catches the most common causes of submission delays.

  5. Materials ready for submission

    You leave with completed credentialing materials, fingerprint cards, drug test documentation, identity forms and any other completed requirements, ready to submit to your employer, agency or contracting organization as instructed. We do not submit on your behalf.

What exactly is vendor credentialing?

Vendor credentialing is a verification and documentation process used by employers, healthcare facilities, staffing agencies and regulated organizations to confirm that individuals or vendors meet their qualification, compliance and safety requirements before granting access, awarding a contract or allowing work to begin. Requirements vary by organization but commonly include background checks, fingerprinting, drug testing, identity verification and review of professional licences or certifications.

What services do you provide for vendor credentialing?

We provide the documentation readiness and background check completion services that make up most credentialing requirements: FD-258 fingerprinting, drug and alcohol testing, identity verification paperwork, professional licence verification support, onboarding packet review and form completion assistance.

Do you approve or deny credentialing applications?

No. We do not approve, deny or adjudicate credentialing decisions. Our role is to help ensure required materials are properly completed, correctly executed and ready for review by the requesting organization. All final decisions are made by the employer, facility or governing authority, not by us.

What should I bring to my credentialing appointment?

Bring your valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID and everything your employer or contracting organization has provided, onboarding packets, checklists, credentialing forms and any specific instructions about what steps are required and how materials should be submitted. If you are unsure what to bring, call us first at (816) 442-0295.

Can you handle credentialing for multiple staff or vendors at once?

Yes. For organizations onboarding multiple employees, contractors or vendors simultaneously, we can schedule group appointments at our office or provide mobile service at your facility across the Kansas City metro. Each individual is processed separately with their own completed documentation.

I am a travel nurse starting a new assignment, can you help?

Yes, travel nurses and contract clinical staff are among the most common people we assist. Bring your agency-provided credentialing checklist and any facility-specific instructions. We will complete fingerprinting, drug testing and documentation review in one appointment so your packet is ready to submit on your timeline. Call ahead so we know what is coming.
