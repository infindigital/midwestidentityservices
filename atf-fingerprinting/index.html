Who needs ATF fingerprinting

Fingerprinting required for all of these

If the ATF requires fingerprints as part of your application, individual, trust or FFL, this is where you go in Kansas City.

NFA Form 1 filers

Applying to make and register an NFA item: SBR, SBS, suppressor or any destructive device. Fingerprints required for individual applicants.

NFA Form 4 filers

Tax-paid transfer of an NFA item. Fingerprints required for individual buyers: an EFT file for eForm 4, or FD-258 cards with a paper application.

NFA Form 5 filers

Tax-exempt transfer applications. Fingerprints required for individual transferees on qualifying Form 5 submissions.

FFL license applicants

Every Responsible Person on an FFL application (ATF Form 7/CR or renewal Form 8) must submit fingerprints on FD-258 using Form 23.

Gun trust members

All Responsible Persons named on a gun trust must submit separate fingerprint submissions with NFA applications. We schedule multi-trustee groups in one appointment.

FFL renewals & changes

Adding a new Responsible Person to an existing FFL, or undergoing a renewal that requires updated fingerprint submissions.

ATF form reference

Which form are you filing?

Quick reference to the most common ATF forms that require fingerprints. Not sure which one applies? Call us before you come in.

  • ATF Form 1: Application to Make & RegisterMost common · NFA

  • Used when you are manufacturing or converting an NFA item yourself

    Building an SBR from a pistol or rifle, a short barreled shotgun (SBS) build, suppressor fabrication (home manufacture), or Any Other Weapon (AOW) manufacture. Required: an EFT file for eForm 1, or two FD-258 cards on paper, per individual applicant or per Responsible Person on a trust.

  • ATF Form 4: Application for Tax Paid TransferMost common · NFA

  • Used when purchasing an NFA item from a dealer or transferring ownership

    Suppressor purchase from a dealer, short barreled rifle purchase or transfer, machine gun transfer (pre-1986 registered), or any NFA item transfer between individuals. The $200 (or $5) tax stamp is paid with this application. Required: an EFT file for eForm 4, or two FD-258 cards on paper, per individual applicant or Responsible Person.

  • ATF Form 5: Tax Exempt TransferNFA

  • Used for tax-exempt transfers of NFA items

    Most commonly for inheritance transfers to an heir, estate transfers, transfers to a government agency, or dealer sample transfers.

  • ATF Form 23: FFL Responsible Person QuestionnaireFFL licensing

  • Required for every Responsible Person on a new FFL application or change

    New FFL application (all owners and Responsible Persons), adding a new Responsible Person to an existing FFL, Type 01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10 and 11 FFLs, and SOT (Special Occupational Taxpayer) applications. Submitted alongside the FD-258 fingerprint submissions.

Not sure which form applies to you? Call us at (816) 442-0295 before coming in. Tell us what you are trying to accomplish, we will confirm the form type and let you know exactly what to bring. Takes two minutes.

What to expect

The ATF fingerprinting process

Most ATF fingerprinting appointments take 15–20 minutes, whether you need an EFT file or FD-258 cards. Here is exactly what happens.

  1. Call ahead to confirm your form type

    We recommend calling (816) 442-0295 before your appointment to confirm which ATF form you are filing: Form 1, Form 4, Form 5 or FFL Form 23. Tell us whether you are filing through ATF eForms or on paper, so we prepare an EFT file or FD-258 cards correctly from the start.

  2. Arrive with your ID and form information

    Bring a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID and any instructions or form numbers provided to you by the ATF or your attorney. No forms to bring, we handle everything in-office.

  3. Capture your prints in the format your form needs

    For eForms we capture your prints digitally and produce an electronic EFT file. For paper filings we complete standard FD-258 cards. Either way we fill in the required applicant fields: name, date of birth, reason fingerprinted and ORI number where applicable.

  4. Every submission reviewed before you leave

    Before you walk out the door, we review each submission for ridge clarity and legibility. If a form needs to be redone, we do it on the spot, before your appointment is considered complete.

  5. Fingerprints ready to submit

    You leave with your EFT file or completed FD-258 cards, ready to include with your ATF application. Submit directly to the ATF or through your dealer or attorney as required by your form.

ATF fingerprinting FAQ

Common questions answered

Still unsure? Call (816) 442-0295 and we will sort it out in two minutes.

ATF eFile (EFT) fingerprinting
I need fingerprints for an ATF eForm. Do I need an EFT file or fingerprint cards?

It depends on how you are filing. ATF eForms submissions use an electronic fingerprint file (EFT), and paper submissions use FD-258 fingerprint cards. Follow the fingerprint instructions for the form you are filing, and if you are unsure, send them to us before your appointment. We offer both at our Kansas City office. Read EFT file or fingerprint cards? or see ATF eFile (EFT) fingerprinting.

Do I need an appointment for ATF fingerprinting?

We recommend calling ahead for ATF fingerprinting, especially for gun trust multi-trustee appointments or FFL applications, so we can confirm your form type and whether you need an EFT file or FD-258 cards. Call (816) 442-0295 or book online before you come in.

How much does ATF fingerprinting cost?

ATF / EFT fingerprinting is $75 per person, whether you need an electronic EFT file or FD-258 cards. For gun trusts with multiple Responsible Persons, each individual is billed separately, and everyone can be seen in a single group appointment.

What is EFT fingerprinting and do you offer it?

Yes, EFT fingerprinting is available now. An EFT is an electronic fingerprint file, the digital format used with ATF eForms submissions such as eForm 1 and eForm 4. Instead of mailing physical FD-258 cards, you upload the electronic file with your eForm. See ATF eFile fingerprinting.

How many FD-258 cards do I need for a paper ATF filing?

For paper filings the ATF asks for two FD-258 cards per individual applicant on Form 1, Form 4 and Form 5, and two per Responsible Person on FFL Form 23. Our $75 fee covers both cards for one person. See FD-258 fingerprinting for the format detail.

Do I file the Form 1 or Form 4 myself, or do you do that?

We provide the fingerprints only, as an EFT file or completed FD-258 cards. The ATF form itself (Form 1, Form 4, Form 23 and so on) is filed by you, your attorney or your dealer, not by us. We handle the fingerprint portion of your application package.

Can all my gun trust members come in together?

Yes. We schedule multi-trustee gun trusts as a single group appointment. All Responsible Persons are fingerprinted together and each receives their own EFT file or FD-258 cards. Plan for approximately 15–20 minutes per person.

Can you fingerprint several Responsible Persons at our FFL or business?

Yes, ask us about a mobile session. We are based in Kansas City and travel within Missouri and Kansas. Mobile pricing is the per-person price plus a travel fee quoted before booking. See mobile fingerprinting.

My Form 4 was rejected due to bad fingerprint submissions. Can you redo them?

Yes. We regularly assist customers whose submissions were rejected elsewhere. Come in, bring the rejection notice if you have it, and we will prepare new prints with extra attention to ridge clarity. Every submission is reviewed before you leave.
