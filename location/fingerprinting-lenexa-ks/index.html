Who we serve in Lenexa

Who we fingerprint in Lenexa

Lenexa’s mix of bioscience, engineering, logistics and healthcare work shapes which fingerprint requests we see most from the city.

Lenexa has one of the densest clusters of laboratory and bioscience employers in the metro, including Clinical Reference Laboratory and Quest Diagnostics. Lab, clinical and healthcare staff often need fingerprints for a license or for a facility credential, and many of them are applying to boards in more than one state. That is where our multi-state print-to-card service saves a second trip.

Engineering and construction firms such as Kiewit have offices in Lenexa, and staff assigned to federal or utility projects are sometimes asked for security clearance fingerprints or contractor badging prints. The warehouses and distribution centers along the I-435 and K-10 corridors add a steady flow of drug testing and employment fingerprinting for new hires.

Families in Lenexa are served by the Shawnee Mission, Olathe and De Soto school districts, so we regularly fingerprint new teachers, substitutes and coaches for Kansas teacher licensing. AdventHealth Lenexa and the larger AdventHealth Shawnee Mission campus in nearby Merriam bring nurses who need Kansas Board of Nursing fingerprints.

Good to know

Lenexa fingerprinting FAQ

More questions? Call (816) 442-0295 or send us your agency’s instructions.

Where can I get fingerprinted near me in Lenexa?

Our office at 8101 E. Bannister Rd, Kansas City, MO is about 20 to 25 minutes from most of Lenexa via I-435 east. We do not have a separate Lenexa office, but our mobile service comes to Lenexa homes, offices and labs for the standard price plus a travel fee quoted before you book.

I work at a Lenexa lab and need prints for a license in another state. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the board’s fingerprint instructions first. If the board accepts FD-258 or its own card, we capture your prints once and print the cards you need, at $60 for Multi-State Print-to-Card plus $25 for each additional printed card. If the board requires its own electronic vendor, we will tell you before you book.

Can you fingerprint a group of new hires at our Lenexa office?

Yes. We run on-site group sessions for Lenexa employers, from a handful of new hires to a full onboarding class. Tell us how many people and what each one needs, and we will quote the session before booking.
