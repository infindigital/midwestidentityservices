Fingerprinting Services

Professional Fingerprinting in Kansas City, Missouri

Whether you need an FBI fingerprinting Kansas City MO submission for a professional license, a federal contract, or a personal background check, Midwest Identity Services provides certified ink fingerprinting on current FD-258 cards, the federally accepted format for the vast majority of government and regulatory submissions. We have been serving Kansas City, Missouri residents and employers from our Bannister Road office for more than five years, and our careful process means every card is prepared, completed, and quality-reviewed before it leaves our office.

Live Scan & FD-258 Ink Fingerprinting

Live Scan fingerprinting Kansas City MO refers to digital fingerprint capture that transmits results electronically to a requesting agency, commonly required by Missouri state agencies and certain licensing boards that use electronic submission pipelines. For the majority of fingerprinting needs in Kansas City, Missouri, including FBI, ATF, FINRA, out-of-state licensing, and most federal regulatory submissions, our FD-258 fingerprint card Kansas City MO service is the correct and most widely accepted format. We always confirm the right method for your specific submission before you arrive. If your agency requires Live Scan electronic transmission, we will direct you to the appropriate vendor and let you know before you make the trip.

FBI Background Check & Identity History Summary

Residents and organizations in Kansas City, Missouri frequently need an FBI background check Kansas City MO for purposes ranging from personal identity verification and apostille requests to employment screening, adoption, and immigration. Our fingerprinting service captures your prints on the standard FD-258 card and prepares them for submission to the FBI Criminal Justice Information Services Division. Where an agency requires electronic submission instead of a physical FD-258 card, we review your submission instructions to confirm the correct method before any collection takes place. For FBI Identity History Summary requests, we capture your fingerprints electronically and submit them for FBI Identity History Summary processing; results are often returned within 24–72 hours of electronic submission, though timing is set by the FBI and can be longer. We also assist with the FBI Identity History Summary Kansas City process, which allows individuals to obtain a copy of their own FBI criminal history record for personal use or disclosure requirements.

Bring your submission instructions. Every agency has slightly different field requirements on the FD-258 card. Bringing the instructions or forms provided by your licensing board, employer, or government agency allows us to complete your card correctly the first time, reducing the chance of rejection.

Immigration Fingerprinting

Kansas City, Missouri has a growing and diverse immigrant population, and immigration fingerprinting Kansas City MO is among our most frequently requested services. Whether you are applying for a visa, green card, citizenship, or completing USCIS-required identity verification, we provide FD-258 ink fingerprinting suitable for immigration-related background check submissions. Immigration fingerprinting requirements can vary by application type and processing office, we encourage you to review your USCIS or consular instructions and bring them to your appointment so we can complete each field correctly.

Federal & USPS Contractor Fingerprinting

Kansas City is a major hub for federal operations, logistics, and government contracting, home to significant USPS facilities, federal offices, and defense-related employers. USPS contractor fingerprinting Kansas City is a common need for mail contractors, rural carriers, and USPS-affiliated vendors who must complete fingerprint-based background checks before being granted access to postal facilities or employment. Similarly, federal contractor fingerprinting Kansas City MO is required for individuals and companies working on federal contracts that carry security, background check, or suitability determination requirements. We provide professional FD-258 fingerprinting for these submissions and work through any agency-specific instructions you provide.

Healthcare & Nursing License Fingerprinting

The Kansas City metro area is home to some of Missouri's largest healthcare systems, including the University of Kansas Health System, Saint Luke's Health System, Children's Mercy Hospital, and Truman Medical Centers. Healthcare fingerprinting Kansas City MO and nursing license fingerprinting Kansas City MO are consistently high-demand services for clinical staff, registered nurses, LPNs, CNAs, allied health professionals, and anyone applying for or renewing a Missouri healthcare license that requires fingerprint-based background check submission. We prepare FD-258 cards per the Missouri Division of Professional Registration or your licensing board's specifications and review every card before you leave.

Childcare Fingerprinting

Missouri law requires fingerprint-based background checks for individuals working in licensed childcare settings, including childcare centers, home daycare providers, and staff at preschool and early childhood programs. Childcare fingerprinting Kansas City MO submissions must meet Missouri Children's Division specifications. We are familiar with these requirements and ensure each card is completed per Missouri's childcare background check standards. Kansas City parents and providers can book quickly, appointments are typically available Monday through Friday and on Saturday mornings.

ATF, FFL & FINRA Fingerprinting

Kansas City, Missouri's financial and firearms industries rely on specialized regulatory fingerprinting. ATF fingerprinting Kansas City MO and FFL fingerprinting Kansas City MO are required for Federal Firearms License applications, renewals, responsible person submissions, and NFA item transactions under ATF Form 5320.23. We provide FD-258 ink fingerprinting completed to ATF-fingerprinting specifications, reviewed for quality before you leave. For the financial industry, FINRA fingerprinting Kansas City MO is required for broker-dealers, registered representatives, and securities professionals submitting Form U4 or Form U5. We follow FINRA's fingerprinting submission standards and accommodate individual and multiple-registrant appointments.

Employment & Group Fingerprinting

Employment fingerprinting Kansas City MO covers the full range of pre-employment and ongoing workforce background check fingerprinting, from individual job applicants to large organizations onboarding entire teams. Group fingerprinting Kansas City MO is one of our most popular employer services: we coordinate appointments for multiple employees from the same organization, either at our Bannister Road office or through mobile fingerprinting at your Kansas City facility. Kansas City area employers in healthcare, education, financial services, logistics, and government contracting regularly rely on our group fingerprinting program to process new hires efficiently without disrupting daily operations.

Drug Testing Services

Certified Drug & Alcohol Testing in Kansas City, MO

Alongside fingerprinting, Midwest Identity Services provides certified drug and alcohol testing in Kansas City, Missouri for employers, individuals, and court-ordered programs. Our testing services cover DOT-compliant and non-DOT programs, using certified chain-of-custody collection procedures, SAMHSA-certified laboratory analysis, and Medical Review Officer (MRO) review on all non-negative results. Whether you are a Kansas City trucking company maintaining FMCSA compliance, an employer building a workplace drug testing program, or an individual required to test by a court or probation officer, we handle every step of the process with the same documented, auditable standards.

DOT Drug & Alcohol Testing

Kansas City, Missouri is one of the nation's central freight and transportation hubs, with thousands of commercial drivers operating under FMCSA jurisdiction throughout the metro area. Our DOT drug and alcohol testing program serves Kansas City carriers and CDL holders with all six federally required test types: pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable suspicion, return-to-duty, and follow-up. All DOT drug testing follows 49 CFR Part 40 procedures with SAMHSA-certified laboratory analysis and MRO review. For Kansas City employers managing random testing pools or FMCSA Clearinghouse query requirements, call us at (816) 442-0295 to discuss your program.

Post-Accident Testing & Urgent DOT Testing

DOT post-accident testing has strict federal time windows, alcohol must be tested within 2 hours of a qualifying accident and drugs within 32 hours. For Kansas City carriers and fleet operators, having our number saved before an accident occurs can be the difference between compliance and a recordable violation. Call us immediately at (816) 442-0295 after any qualifying accident and we will confirm your testing windows and get your driver in as fast as possible.

Non-DOT Employer & Individual Drug Testing

For Kansas City, Missouri employers who are not DOT-regulated, we provide 5-panel, 9-panel, and 10-panel urine drug testing with certified chain-of-custody collection and MRO-reviewed results. Pre-employment screening, random workplace programs, post-accident investigation, and reasonable suspicion testing are all available for Kansas City area businesses. Kansas City's largest employment sectors, healthcare, logistics, construction, financial services, and education, each have specific drug testing considerations, and we work with employers across all of them. Court-ordered and probation drug testing is also available for Kansas City residents required to test by a Missouri court or probation officer.
