A common question from security companies

“Our security installers need licensing in Missouri, Kansas, Texas and Florida. Can one fingerprint appointment cover multiple states?”

Often, yes, for much of it. One digital capture can produce multiple fingerprint cards when permitted by each receiving agency. Some states use a different submission method, so we review each state’s instructions before the session.

  1. You send us each state’s instructions

    Forward the licensing board or agency instructions, ORI numbers and card requirements for every state your installers will work in. We check which states accept FD-258 or agency-specific cards and which require something else.

  2. One digital capture per installer

    Each installer is fingerprinted once on our digital print-to-card system. From that capture we can produce multiple FD-258 or agency-specific cards for the states that accept printed cards, when permitted by each receiving agency.

  3. Florida handled electronically where required

    Florida may require electronic submission to FDLE rather than a card. We offer Florida Live Scan submitted electronically from Missouri, usually in the same visit.

  4. Texas and other states follow their own rules

    Texas and other states have their own fingerprinting requirements and submission methods. Where a state requires its own process, we tell you up front so nothing is missed at the session.

Every licensing agency makes its own decision about the fingerprints it accepts, and we cannot guarantee acceptance by any agency. What we can do is review each state’s instructions first and prepare what each one asks for. Learn more about multi-state fingerprinting and print-to-card.

Alarm & security fingerprinting FAQ

Do alarm and security installers need fingerprints?

In many states, alarm and security installers, low-voltage technicians and often door-to-door sales agents must be licensed and fingerprinted before they can work. Requirements vary by state and city, so send us your instructions and we will handle the fingerprinting and background screening for your team.

Our security installers need licensing in Missouri, Kansas, Texas and Florida. Can one fingerprint appointment cover multiple states?

Often it can cover much of it. One digital capture can produce multiple fingerprint cards when permitted by each receiving agency. Florida may require electronic submission to FDLE, which we offer through Florida Live Scan, and Texas and other states have their own requirements. We review each state’s instructions before the session and prepare what each one asks for, but acceptance is always the receiving agency’s decision and we cannot guarantee it. See multi-state fingerprinting.

Can you fingerprint our whole sales team or crew at once?

Yes. We run on-site group fingerprinting sessions at your office or training site, processing your whole cohort in one visit with FD-258 cards, print-to-card and electronic Live Scan available depending on what each state requires.

How much does group or mobile fingerprinting cost for our installers?

Each installer is billed at the per-person price for the service they need: FD-258 fingerprinting is $45 ($20 each additional card), multi-state print-to-card is $60 ($25 each additional printed card), and Florida FDLE Live Scan is $65 plus the Florida ORI fee. On-site sessions add a travel fee quoted before booking.

We hire hundreds of reps for a summer sales program. Can you keep up?

Yes. Pest control, solar and door-to-door sales programs that onboard reps in large seasonal waves are exactly what a managed compliance account is built for. We set up a workflow around your onboarding dates and hiring volume and run on-site group fingerprinting sessions as cohorts come in, with one consolidated account and a single point of contact throughout the season.

Do you offer notary services for our hiring documents?

Yes. Alongside fingerprinting, we offer in-house notary service so your hiring and licensing documents get notarized on site. Drug testing is available as well, so your group sessions can cover fingerprinting, notarization and drug testing in a single visit.
