Why businesses bring us in

Bring on every vendor with confidence

When you bring an outside contractor, vendor or agency worker on-site, part of the job is making sure they have been properly vetted, and being able to show it if a regulator, client or insurer ever asks.

It gets easier with one consistent process behind it. Instead of screening handled piece by piece, one vendor fingerprinted here, a drug test there, results scattered across inboxes, we give every vendor the same complete, compliant screening, organized and ready the moment you need to produce it.

How it works

From setup to cleared vendor, four steps

One consistent, repeatable process for every contractor you bring on, from the first conversation to audit-ready documentation.

  1. Scope your program

    We review your facility’s requirements, credentialing-platform checklist or compliance standards and confirm the exact checks, formats and routing your vendors need, before you open the account, not after the first submission bounces.

  2. Open a business account

    We set up a recurring account with volume pricing locked in, no per-appointment rate negotiation every time you onboard a new contractor cohort. One invoice, one point of contact, every vendor.

  3. Screen each vendor

    Vendors come to our Kansas City location or we come to your site. Fingerprints, drug tests, background checks and I-9 verification, handled in a single appointment where possible. No sending people to separate locations.

  4. Receive audit-ready results

    Every submission reviewed before transmission. Results delivered in the format your credentialing platform or compliance team requires, organized, complete and ready to stand behind when a regulator, client or insurer asks.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

More questions? Call (816) 442-0295 and we will answer in two minutes.

Can you handle ongoing, high-volume screening for our entire vendor base?

Yes. Recurring business accounts are built for exactly this, whether you onboard a handful of contractors a month or run large cohorts. We standardize the process so each new vendor follows the same compliant path, with pricing that scales to your volume rather than being billed at per-appointment rates.

Can you screen vendors with requirements in multiple states?

Yes. We offer Florida Live Scan (FDLE electronic submission) for Florida requirements, FD-258 card fingerprinting for states and boards that require physical cards, multi-state print-to-card that produces multiple cards from one capture when permitted by the receiving agency, and FBI Identity History Summary electronic submission, so a multi-market contractor base does not require multiple providers. We review each state’s instructions first.

Do you work with our credentialing platform and facility requirements?

Yes. Send us your facility’s requirements or platform checklist and we will complete and verify the specific fingerprinting, testing and background components it calls for, formatted and routed correctly. We confirm the exact configuration before opening your account so there are no surprises at the point of submission. Healthcare facilities can also see healthcare fingerprinting.

Can you come to our facility for group screening?

Yes. Our mobile team covers the Kansas City metro, and other locations in Missouri and Kansas by arrangement, and coordinates on-site appointments for contractor groups, new-hire cohorts and recurring onboarding. Mobile pricing is the per-person price plus a travel fee quoted before booking.

Do you decide whether a vendor passes their background check?

No. We deliver complete, accurate, compliant screening results, fingerprints captured and transmitted correctly, background checks assembled to the levels you require, drug tests processed and routed properly. Hiring, access and credentialing decisions stay with your organization or its platform. Our job is to make sure the file you are deciding on is done right.
