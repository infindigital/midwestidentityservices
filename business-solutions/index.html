Do you offer volume pricing?

Yes. Contact us at (816) 442-0295 to discuss your monthly hiring volume and we will put together a custom rate. Most volume agreements are confirmed same day.

How fast do your pre-employment packages turn around?

Most package results are returned within 1–3 business days, depending on the searches and jurisdictions involved. Expedited options are available for time-sensitive hires, and Essential packages often return same or next business day. For fingerprint-based checks, FBI Identity History Summary results are often returned within 24–72 hours of electronic submission; timing is set by the FBI and can be longer.

Are your packages FCRA-compliant?

Yes. All pre-employment screening programs include FCRA-compliant disclosure forms, authorization forms and adverse action procedures, pre-adverse and final adverse action letters are included.

Which industries do you have fingerprinting programs for?

We have dedicated pages for healthcare, nursing schools, financial services & FINRA, security & alarm, transportation & USPS contractors, staffing & corporate and ATF & firearms. Each explains who needs prints, which method applies, pricing and what to bring.

Our staff need licenses in more than one state. Can one appointment cover them?

Often it can. Our digital print-to-card system captures fingerprints once and produces multiple FD-258 or agency-specific cards when permitted by the receiving agency. Some states require their own electronic submission, so we review each state’s instructions first. See multi-state fingerprinting.

Can you come to our office for fingerprinting?

Yes. We offer mobile on-site fingerprinting across the Kansas City metro, and elsewhere in Missouri and Kansas by arrangement, for batch hires, orientations and HR compliance events. Mobile pricing is the per-person price plus a travel fee quoted before booking.

Do you support DOT-regulated companies?

Yes. We offer full DOT drug and alcohol compliance programs for FMCSA-regulated carriers, including random pool management, Clearinghouse queries and PSP reports.

We are an FFL dealer. Can we partner with you for ATF fingerprinting?

Yes. We offer FFL dealer partnership programs for ATF fingerprinting referrals, with EFT files and FD-258 cards both available at $75 per person. Call (816) 442-0295 to discuss how we can support your customers who need ATF Form 1, Form 4 or FFL fingerprinting.

What information do we need to get started?

Your company name, number of employees or expected monthly hire volume, and the types of roles you are screening for. Call (816) 442-0295 and we can have a proposal to you the same day.
