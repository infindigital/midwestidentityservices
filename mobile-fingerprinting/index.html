About this service

Certified fingerprinting delivered to your location

The same certified technicians, the same quality standards and the same review process, just at your location instead of ours.

Mobile fingerprinting means we bring our full certified fingerprinting equipment, including both Live Scan digital scanners and FD-258 ink fingerprinting supplies, directly to your home, office, warehouse, clinic, school or event venue anywhere across the Kansas City metro area.

The process is identical to our in-office service. Every print is checked before we pack up and leave.

Team members applying for licenses in more than one state? Ask about Multi-State Print-to-Card, which captures fingerprints once and produces multiple FD-258 or agency-specific cards when permitted by the receiving agency ($60, plus $25 for each additional printed card). Executives who need an FBI Identity History Summary can be fingerprinted at the office too: see mobile FBI Identity History Summary for executives.

Best for groups of 5 or more.

Mobile service is most cost-effective for groups of 5 or more people at the same location. For individuals or smaller groups, visiting our office at 8101 E. Bannister Rd is usually faster and more affordable. Call us and we will help you decide which option makes more sense.

How it works

Scheduling mobile fingerprinting, step by step

What the process looks like from first contact to completed prints.

  1. Call or email to discuss your needs

    Contact us at (816) 442-0295 or through our contact form. Tell us how many people need fingerprinting, the type required (Live Scan, FD-258 or both) and your location. We will confirm availability and provide a travel fee quote upfront, no surprises.

  2. Confirm your appointment & location details

    We confirm the date, time, address, number of participants and any special requirements. For business bookings, we coordinate with your HR or operations contact to make sure the space is ready and participants are scheduled correctly.

  3. We arrive with full equipment

    Our certified technician arrives with all equipment: Live Scan scanner, FD-258 ink supplies, intake forms and ID verification materials. All you need to provide is a table, two chairs and adequate lighting.

  4. Each participant is processed individually

    Each person presents their valid photo ID, completes the necessary intake information and has their fingerprints captured. For FD-258 submissions, each form is reviewed for quality before moving to the next participant. For Live Scan, the digital submission is confirmed before proceeding.

  5. Completed submissions delivered on-site

    FD-258 participants receive their completed, reviewed submissions on the spot. Live Scan participants receive their submission confirmation. For business clients, we can provide a summary of all participants processed and submission statuses before we leave.

Mobile fingerprinting FAQ

Frequently asked questions

More questions? Call (816) 442-0295 and we will answer right away.

How far will you travel for mobile fingerprinting?

We regularly serve the full Kansas City metro including Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa, Shawnee, Independence, Lee’s Summit, Blue Springs, Liberty and surrounding areas. For locations further out, call us at (816) 442-0295 with your address and group size, we can often accommodate with an adjusted travel fee.

What is the minimum group size for mobile service?

There is no hard minimum, but mobile service is most cost-effective for groups of 5 or more. For 1–4 people, visiting our office at 8101 E. Bannister Rd is usually faster and more affordable. We can help you decide, just call and we will be honest about which option makes more sense.

Can you do both Live Scan and FD-258 at the same appointment?

Yes. We bring both Live Scan digital equipment and FD-258 ink fingerprinting supplies to every mobile appointment. If your group has people needing different submission types, for example, some need Live Scan for FBI background checks and others need FD-258 for a state board, we can handle both in a single visit.

How much does mobile fingerprinting cost?

Each person pays the individual service price: FD-258 fingerprinting $45 ($20 each additional card), Multi-State Print-to-Card $60 ($25 each additional printed card), FBI Identity History Summary Electronic Submission $90 or ATF / EFT fingerprinting $75. A travel fee based on your distance from our office is quoted before booking, and group rates are quoted per session. We confirm the full cost before your appointment is booked, no surprises. Call (816) 442-0295 with your location and group size for an immediate quote.

What space do you need at our location?

A table, two chairs and good lighting, that is all. Any quiet indoor space works: a conference room, break room, office or lobby. We have also worked in covered outdoor spaces. We set up and break down our own equipment completely.

How long does a mobile appointment take?

Allow approximately 15–20 minutes per person, plus setup and breakdown time of about 15 minutes. For a group of 10, plan for roughly 3–4 hours total including a brief setup at the start and wrap-up at the end. We will give you a time estimate based on your group size when you book.

Can you come to a home for an individual who cannot travel?

Yes. We regularly visit private residences for individuals who are homebound due to disability, illness, mobility limitations or caregiver responsibilities. The appointment works exactly the same way, we bring everything we need and only require a small clear space to work. Call (816) 442-0295 to arrange a home visit.

Do you serve businesses outside Kansas City?

Our primary coverage area is the Kansas City metro. For locations further away in Missouri or Kansas, call us with your address and group size. For large groups, we can sometimes make extended-distance trips work. We will always give you an honest answer about whether the logistics make sense.
