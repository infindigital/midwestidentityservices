Who needs ATF fingerprinting

Fingerprinting required for all of these

If the ATF requires fingerprints as part of your application, individual, trust or FFL, this is where you go in Kansas City.

NFA Form 1 filers

Applying to make and register an NFA item: SBR, SBS, suppressor or any destructive device. Fingerprints required for individual applicants.

NFA Form 4 filers

Tax-paid transfer of an NFA item. Fingerprints required for individual buyers. Two FD-258 submissions included with the transfer application.

NFA Form 5 filers

Tax-exempt transfer applications. Fingerprints required for individual transferees on qualifying Form 5 submissions.

FFL license applicants

Every Responsible Person on an FFL application (ATF Form 7/CR or renewal Form 8) must submit fingerprints on FD-258 using Form 23.

Gun trust members

All Responsible Persons named on a gun trust must submit separate fingerprint submissions with NFA applications. We schedule multi-trustee groups in one appointment.

FFL renewals & changes

Adding a new Responsible Person to an existing FFL, or undergoing a renewal that requires updated fingerprint submissions.

ATF form reference

Which form are you filing?

Quick reference to the most common ATF forms that require fingerprints. Not sure which one applies? Call us before you come in.

  • ATF Form 1: Application to Make & RegisterMost common · NFA

  • Used when you are manufacturing or converting an NFA item yourself

    Building an SBR from a pistol or rifle, a short barreled shotgun (SBS) build, suppressor fabrication (home manufacture), or Any Other Weapon (AOW) manufacture. Required: two FD-258 submissions per individual applicant or per Responsible Person on a trust.

  • ATF Form 4: Application for Tax Paid TransferMost common · NFA

  • Used when purchasing an NFA item from a dealer or transferring ownership

    Suppressor purchase from a dealer, short barreled rifle purchase or transfer, machine gun transfer (pre-1986 registered), or any NFA item transfer between individuals. The $200 (or $5) tax stamp is paid with this application. Required: two FD-258 submissions per individual applicant or Responsible Person.

  • ATF Form 5: Tax Exempt TransferNFA

  • Used for tax-exempt transfers of NFA items

    Most commonly for inheritance transfers to an heir, estate transfers, transfers to a government agency, or dealer sample transfers.

  • ATF Form 23: FFL Responsible Person QuestionnaireFFL licensing

  • Required for every Responsible Person on a new FFL application or change

    New FFL application (all owners and Responsible Persons), adding a new Responsible Person to an existing FFL, Type 01, 02, 03, 06, 07, 08, 09, 10 and 11 FFLs, and SOT (Special Occupational Taxpayer) applications. Submitted alongside the FD-258 fingerprint submissions.

Not sure which form applies to you? Call us at (816) 442-0295 before coming in. Tell us what you are trying to accomplish, we will confirm the form type and let you know exactly what to bring. Takes two minutes.

What to expect

The ATF fingerprinting process

Most ATF fingerprinting appointments take 15–20 minutes. Here is exactly what happens.

  1. Call ahead to confirm your form type

    We recommend calling (816) 442-0295 before your appointment to confirm which ATF form you are filing: Form 1, Form 4, Form 5 or FFL Form 23. This ensures we prepare the submissions correctly from the start.

  2. Arrive with your ID and form information

    Bring a valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID and any instructions or form numbers provided to you by the ATF or your attorney. No forms to bring, we handle everything in-office.

  3. Complete the FD-258 submissions to ATF specifications

    We capture your prints using traditional ink on standard FD-258 forms and fill in all required applicant identifier fields, name, date of birth, reason fingerprinted and ORI number where applicable.

  4. Every submission reviewed before you leave

    Before you walk out the door, we review each submission for ridge clarity and legibility. If a form needs to be redone, we do it on the spot, before your appointment is considered complete.

  5. Fingerprints in hand, ready to submit

    You leave with your completed FD-258 submissions, ready to include with your ATF application package. Submit directly to the ATF or through your dealer or attorney as required by your form.

ATF eFile fingerprinting
Do I need an appointment for ATF fingerprinting?

We recommend calling ahead for ATF fingerprinting, especially for gun trust multi-trustee appointments or FFL applications, so we can confirm your form type and prepare accordingly. Call (816) 442-0295 before you come in.

How much does ATF fingerprinting cost?

ATF fingerprinting is $60–$99 based on services needed. For gun trusts with multiple Responsible Persons, each individual is billed separately. All Responsible Persons can be seen in a single group appointment.

How many FD-258 submissions do I need?

The ATF requires two FD-258 submissions per individual applicant for Form 1, Form 4 and Form 5 applications. For FFL Form 23, each Responsible Person submits two. Our fee covers both submissions for a single transaction. See FD-258 ink card fingerprinting for the format detail.

Do I file the Form 1 or Form 4 myself, or do you do that?

We provide the completed FD-258 fingerprint submissions only. The ATF form itself (Form 1, Form 4, Form 23 and so on) is filed by you, your attorney or your dealer, not by us. We handle the fingerprint portion of your application package.

Can all my gun trust members come in together?

Yes. We schedule multi-trustee gun trusts as a single group appointment. All Responsible Persons are fingerprinted together and each receives their own separate FD-258 submissions. Plan for approximately 15–20 minutes per person.

What is EFT fingerprinting and when will it be available?

EFT (Electronic Fingerprint Transaction) is a digital fingerprint file format accepted by the ATF’s online eForms system for Form 1 and Form 4 submissions. Instead of physical FD-258 forms, an encrypted digital file is uploaded directly to ATF eForms. We are launching this capability soon, see ATF eFile fingerprinting.

My Form 4 was rejected due to bad fingerprint submissions. Can you redo them?

Yes. We regularly assist customers whose submissions were rejected elsewhere. Come in, bring the rejection notice if you have it, and we will prepare new submissions with extra attention to ridge clarity. Every submission is reviewed before you leave.
